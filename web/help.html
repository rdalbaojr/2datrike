2DA Support Center

Why do I need to input my TODA?

2DA is built to empower local communities. By selecting your specific TODA during registration, your ride requests are instantly routed to the drivers closest to you. This guarantees faster pickups, seamless local communication, and directly supports the tricycle drivers in your neighborhood!

How does the Night Differential work?

To ensure drivers are available for emergency rides during late hours, a 10% Night Rate premium is automatically applied to all bookings made between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.
